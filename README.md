# ALIPEX — Your Financial Lounge
## Talking Money. Building the Next Generation of Financial Intelligence.

What if understanding your money was as natural as having a conversation?

What if your financial assistant could understand your situation, remember your goals and become more useful as your financial life evolves?

That is the future we are building with **ALIPEX**.

## Our Ambition — The ChatGPT of Finance

ALIPEX is an African-founded AI financial intelligence product with a global ambition: to become a trusted conversational interface for personal and business finance.

We want to move beyond recording transactions towards helping people understand their finances, anticipate challenges and take informed action.

**We are starting with English.** Our first priority is to build an excellent English-language experience, establish reliability and validate usefulness before expanding into additional languages.

## We Have Already Started

We have developed an initial working prototype. We are inviting builders to help refine that foundation and turn an ambitious vision into a dependable product.

This repository is our public community hub for collaboration, feedback and development planning. Production source code is not currently published here.

## What We Are Building Towards

### 1. Financial Conversations
Ask questions about spending, budgets, savings and financial commitments—and receive clear answers grounded in your available financial information.

### 2. Intelligence That Learns Over Time
Develop personalisation through user-approved memory, financial history and feedback. Improve models through measured evaluation and controlled updates, keeping personal memory separate from model training.

### 3. Deep Learning for Financial Understanding
Combine advanced language models, deep learning, financial calculations and structured data to identify patterns, interpret context and generate useful insights.

### 4. Financial Behaviour and Creditworthiness
Build consent-based financial profiles that help users understand their financial health and could support creditworthiness assessment.

Develop explainable credit-risk models using authorised data, with validation, fairness testing and human review for consequential lending decisions.

### 5. Proactive Financial Support
Recognise emerging pressures, track financial goals and suggest practical next steps that users can understand and choose.

### 6. Financial Actions
Progressively enable user-authorised payments, transfers and investment services through appropriate financial partners, with clear permissions and confirmation controls.

These capabilities form our development roadmap. They are not all available in the current prototype.

## Our First Build Milestone

Make the English-language financial conversation experience accurate, useful and easy to use.

Our immediate priorities are:

- Reliable transaction capture and categorisation.
- Accurate spending summaries and budget calculations.
- Clear conversational answers grounded in user data.
- User-controlled memory and personalisation.
- A refined mobile experience.
- Reproducible tests for financial accuracy and AI behaviour.
- Privacy, security and dependable operation.
